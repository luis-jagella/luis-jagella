## About me

I am a **Backend Developer with 3 years of professional experience**, focused on turning complex business rules into reliable, readable, and maintainable systems.

I currently work primarily with **InterSystems Caché ObjectScript (MUMPS)**, my main professional development language, while deepening my expertise in the **Java and Spring Boot** ecosystem. I hold a degree in **Systems Analysis and Development** from Católica de Santa Catarina and am pursuing a postgraduate degree in **Software Engineering with Java and AI** at UNIPDS.

<details>
  <summary><strong>What I am building and learning</strong></summary>
  <br />

  - Backend architecture with **Java, Spring Boot, and Quarkus**;
  - Asynchronous integrations and event-driven systems with **Kafka**;
  - Practical **AI** integrations in Java applications;
  - **MyTasks**, a full-stack platform designed to simplify routines with a scalable Java backend and React frontend;
  - More productive development workflows with **Linux, WSL2, Ubuntu, and Zsh**.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>How I build software</strong></summary>
  <br />

  I value purposeful code: clear naming, well-defined responsibilities, tests where they matter, and architectural decisions that make software easier to maintain. A good solution should work today and remain understandable tomorrow.
</details>
